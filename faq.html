Frequently Asked Questions!

What kind of trading systems does MarsQuant build? +

We build end-to-end algorithmic trading systems - including backtesting tools, live market simulators, execution platforms, and automation frameworks - all designed for high performance and scalability.

Do you provide customized trading infrastructure? +

Yes. We design fully custom systems tailored to each client’s strategy, API, and performance needs - from broker integrations to complete algo setups.

Can I test strategies before going live? +

Absolutely. Our backtesting and live market simulator let you validate and refine your trading ideas safely using real or historical market data.
