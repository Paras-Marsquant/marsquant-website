Frequently Asked
Questions

Find answers to common questions about MarsQuant, our trading technology, solutions, and services.

What kind of trading systems does MarsQuant build?

We build end-to-end algorithmic trading systems — including backtesting tools, live market simulators, execution platforms, and automation frameworks — designed for performance and scalability.

Who can use MarsQuant solutions?

Our solutions are designed for professional traders, brokers, proprietary trading desks, and institutions. We work with teams to understand their trading workflows and requirements.

Which markets and instruments do your systems support?

Market and instrument support depends on your data sources, broker connectivity, and use case. We can discuss the required coverage as part of defining your solution.

Can I test strategies before going live?

Yes. Backtesting helps evaluate strategies against historical market data, while the live market simulator lets teams practice in a simulated environment before live execution.

Do you provide customized trading infrastructure?

Yes. Infrastructure can be designed around a team's performance, deployment, data, and operational requirements.

Where do your systems run — cloud or co-location?

Deployment options depend on the solution and its latency, connectivity, and operational needs. Contact us to discuss the right environment for your setup.

How do you keep execution fast and reliable?

Our systems are engineered with a focus on low-latency execution, monitoring, and reliable infrastructure. The specific design depends on the trading workflow and deployment requirements.

Is our strategy logic and data kept private?

Data handling and access requirements are discussed during solution planning so the implementation can reflect your organization's privacy and security needs.

Which brokers and APIs do you integrate with?

Broker and API integrations depend on your requirements and the available interfaces. Share your preferred brokers and data providers with our team so we can assess the integration.

Can your systems connect with our existing tools and data providers?

Existing systems and providers can be reviewed during solution scoping. Integration depends on their interfaces, data formats, and operational requirements.

How long does it take to build and deploy a system?

Timelines vary with the scope, integrations, and infrastructure involved. We establish an estimated delivery plan after understanding your requirements.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Support arrangements can be discussed based on your deployment and operational needs. Contact us to talk through the options for your team.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the solution scope, integrations, and infrastructure requirements. Contact us with your use case for a discussion.
